# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes constructive, good-faith participation. Keep discussion focused on the work, respect people’s time and privacy, and do not post credentials, private media, customer data, or personal information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intentionally unsafe disclosure are not acceptable. Use GitHub's report controls where available, or open a minimal issue requesting a private contact channel for sensitive conduct concerns. Do not disclose private details publicly.

Maintainers may remove content or limit participation when needed to protect people, project discussions, or local-user safety.
